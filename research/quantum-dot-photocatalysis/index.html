Photocatalysis

Single-Atom-Modified Quantum Dots for Coupled Photocatalysis

Investigating Au and Pd single-atom sites on CdS/ZnS quantum dots for coupled CO₂ reduction and organic oxidation.

Scientific motivation

Coupling CO₂ reduction with organic oxidation gives photogenerated electrons and holes separate reaction pathways. This work investigates how isolated metal sites might promote the two half-reactions.

My contributions

Synthesized Au- and Pd-single-atom-modified CdS/ZnS quantum dots, assessed photocatalytic activity by gas chromatography, and characterized alcohol oxidation products with NMR spectroscopy.

Methods

  • Synthesis of single-atom-modified CdS/ZnS quantum dots
  • Photocatalytic activity measurements and gas chromatography
  • NMR spectroscopy for oxidation-product characterization

Selected results & status

The roles of Au and Pd in the two half-reactions are under investigation. Performance data and mechanistic conclusions will be added when available.
