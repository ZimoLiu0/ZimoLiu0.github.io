Electrochemistry

In Situ PLD-EIS for Oxygen Exchange Kinetics

Tracking how PrOₓ surface decoration affects oxygen exchange on LSCF electrodes during thin-film experiments.

Scientific motivation

Surface chemistry influences oxygen exchange at oxide electrodes. Combining pulsed laser deposition and electrochemical impedance spectroscopy makes it possible to monitor electrochemical changes within the deposition chamber.

My contributions

Helped build and optimize a PLD system with integrated real-time EIS.

Measured LSCF electrodes with and without PrOₓ surface modification under anodic and cathodic bias and analyzed concentration-amended activation energies.

Methods

  • In situ pulsed laser deposition with electrochemical impedance spectroscopy
  • Biased oxygen-exchange measurements on decorated and undecorated LSCF
  • Concentration-amended activation energy analysis

Selected results & status

The analysis indicated a lower activation barrier for PrOₓ-decorated LSCF under the conditions studied. Quantitative plots and experimental details can be added when available.
